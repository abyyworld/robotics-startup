# Halal policy (draft)

## Customers
- No weapons makers, and no makers of haram goods (alcohol, pork, gambling and similar).
- Food lines: the halal knife stroke and tasmiyah stay human. Avoid lines that also handle pork or alcohol.

## Money
- Accept: grants, prizes, ordinary shares, and SAFEs only after a scholar has reviewed the **whole** document.
- Refuse: interest-bearing notes, equipment or venture debt, liquidation preferences, cumulative dividends, ratchets.
- Turn off any interest-earning bank sweep from day one.
- Late payments: a scholar-reviewed fixed fee or actual-cost clause instead of interest. Never claim interest.
- Fleet finance: customer-funded (staged payments) or equity first; later ijara or a co-owned fleet company (musharaka).

Get a qualified scholar's written opinion before labelling anything "halal".
