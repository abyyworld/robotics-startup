# Data policy (draft)

1. **Purpose:** data is collected only to teach and supervise the robot on a named task.
2. **Capture:** cameras point at hands and the work area. No faces, no audio.
3. **Consent:** worker opt-in is an ethical safeguard on top of the employer's legal basis and impact assessment. Workers who say no get an alternative with no penalty.
4. **No scoring:** never measure worker productivity or emotions.
5. **Ownership:** robot logs and model improvements can be used across customers (anonymised). Raw customer video and recipes need a separate written agreement.
6. **Retention:** raw video is deleted after training unless the customer agrees otherwise.
7. **Storage:** customer data never goes in the public repo.

Before any real deployment, have this checked by a lawyer for the relevant country (UK GDPR / US state law).
